2. The 9 Core Discovery Areas (What Reps Must Understand)
Discovery Area Trigger Question to Ask (Verbatim Script) Operational Focus & Notes
1. Parcel Volumes "Roughly how many parcels are you dispatching on an average day or week? Does that volume spike significantly during peak periods or promotions?" Establish baseline daily/weekly parcel volume and seasonal surge capacity.
2. Destinations "Where do these orders go? Are you primarily UK domestic mainland, or do you send to the Scottish Highlands, Islands, Northern Ireland, or Europe?" Identify regional domestic distribution and cross-border customs requirements.
3. Recipient Profile "Who receives the goods — direct to consumer (B2C) residential addresses and lockers, or B2B trade and commercial retail addresses?" Matches parcel profile to carrier capabilities (e.g. InPost lockers vs B2B trade).
4. Sizes & Deadweights "What does your parcel profile look like in terms of size and deadweight? What are your typical minimums and maximums?" Examine deadweight vs volumetric limits to prevent carrier surcharge penalties.
5. International Shipping "Do you ship internationally, either to Europe or the rest of the world? How do you currently handle customs and IOSS?" Positions DHL Express / eCommerce capabilities and customs compliance.
6. Hazardous / Special Cargo "Do you send anything that is hazardous, liquids, creams, glass, aerosols, batteries, or anything outside of the norm?" Identifies dangerous goods (ADR) compliance and restricted carrier items.
7. Packaging Requirements "Do you have any specific packaging requirements — for example, poly mailers, custom branded boxes, long tubes, or 6x4 thermal labels?" Ensures packaging format compatibility with carrier sortation belts.
8. Software & Booking Method "How do you book your parcels right now? Are you booking them one by one? What channels (Shopify, Amazon)? If you use a WMS, which one?" Key Software Trigger: If booking 1-by-1, Moov Ninja automates this completely.
9. Couriers, Cut-offs & Pain "Which couriers are collecting today? What time would you need a vehicle to collect from you if you were to move? Any challenges with price, service, or damage?" Cut-Off Trigger: If driver collects too early, switch carriers. Isolate service pain.
