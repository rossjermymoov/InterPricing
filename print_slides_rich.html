Discovery Execution

The 9 Core Discovery Areas

What the sales representative must understand from the business before presenting a proposal.
1. Parcel Volumes

"How many parcels do you send daily and weekly?"

Check baseline volume and peak surge multiples (Black Friday/Q4).

2. Destinations

"Where do you send them to?"

UK domestic mainland, Scottish Highlands/Islands, Northern Ireland, Europe, or ROW.

3. Recipient Profile

"Are they businesses or consumers?"

B2C residential addresses & lockers vs B2B trade retail addresses.

4. Parcel Sizes & Weights

"What are the sizes and deadweights?"

Typical dimensions, minimums, maximums, and volumetric profiles.

5. International Shipping

"Do you send internationally?"

European delivery (IOSS setup) or Rest of World express/standard freight.

6. Hazardous / Special Cargo

"Do you send anything hazardous?"

Liquids, creams, glass, aerosols, batteries, or goods outside the norm.

7. Packaging Requirements

"Any specific packaging rules?"

Poly mailer bags, custom boxes, crash-locks, or 6x4 thermal label printers.

8. Software & Booking

"How do you book parcels now?"

1-by-1 manual entry? Channels (Shopify, Amazon, TikTok)? Any WMS system?

9. Couriers, Cut-offs & Pain

"What collection time do you need?"

Current couriers, required vehicle collection time, and challenges (price, service, damage).

Discovery Objective: Gather complete operational context so you can pivot naturally into booking the live Moov Ninja demo.
