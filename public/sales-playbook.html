Question 2.1 • Deadweight vs Volumetric
"What does your typical box look like? Are you shipping lightweight bulky boxes or heavy compact items?"
Merchants often don't realise they are paying volumetric penalties (e.g., a 600g item in a 40cm box billed at 4kg). DPD and UPS have differing volumetric divisor formulas.
Small Packet (< 1kg) Standard Shoebox (1–3kg) Bulky / High-Cube (> 5kg vol) Heavy Freight (> 20kg)
Question 2.2 • Packaging Format & Automation
"Are you using poly mailer flyer bags, custom branded crash-lock boxes, or varied outer cartons?"
Poly mailers often catch on standard conveyor belts unless sent with carriers optimised for soft freight (e.g. InPost / Evri / DPD Packet).
Poly Mailers / Flyer Bags Branded Cardboard Cartons Irregular / Long Tubes
Question 2.3 • Regulated & Special Cargo
"Do you despatch liquids, cosmetic creams, glass, lithium batteries, or items with specific ADR carriage rules?"
Carriers have rigid restricted goods policies. Misdeclaring liquids or batteries can lead to destroyed shipments and account suspensions.
Standard Clean Cargo Cosmetics / Liquids Fragile Glass / Ceramics Lithium Batteries / Tech

📦 Packaging Optimization Value

  • Volumetric Rescue: Match bulky lightweight parcels to carriers with friendlier volumetric divisors (saving up to 35% per parcel).
  • Drop-to-Shop & Locker Match: For smaller items, position InPost lockers to reduce door-to-door failed delivery rates to 0%.
  • Damage Mitigation: Audit packing methods to drastically lower carrier dispute rates.
